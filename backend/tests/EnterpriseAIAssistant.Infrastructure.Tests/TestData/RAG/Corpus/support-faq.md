# Support FAQ — Demo Account

## Resetting a password

To reset a password, the user must open the Demo Portal login page and select **Forgot Password**.

The system sends a password reset link to the email address registered to the account. The link expires after 15 minutes.

If the user does not receive the email, they should check the spam folder and verify the registered email address.

## Account lockout

An account is temporarily locked after five consecutive failed login attempts.

The lockout lasts 10 minutes. After this period, the user can try to log in again.

Contacting support does not automatically unlock the account.

## Support availability

Demo Portal support is available Monday through Friday, from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC.

The support email for this fictional product is support@demo-portal.example.

## Changing the registered email

To change the registered email address, the user must contact support and provide their account identifier. The change requires manual verification by the support team.
